# 📱 Ephemeral Chat: Master Your Privacy

Welcome to the official user guide for **Ephemeral Chat**. This app operates entirely differently from standard messaging apps. It is designed for total anonymity and peace of mind. Every message you send is like writing in the sand at low tide—it’s only there for the moment, and leaves absolutely no traceback to you.

---

## 🚀 The Basics: Getting In

You don't need an account, an email, or even a real name. Just a portal.

### 1. Creating a Room (The Host)
*   Tap **"Create New Room"** on the home screen.
*   **Pick a Nickname**: This is how others will see you for this session. It does not carry over.
*   **Set a Password (Optional)**: Highly recommended. Anyone with the room link will need this password.
    > [!TIP]
    > **Stealth Entry**: When typing your password, you won't see dots or characters appear on screen. This is a security feature to prevent anyone looking over your shoulder from seeing how many characters your password has! Keep typing and hit join.

### 2. Joining a Room (The Guest)
*   **Via Link**: Simply click the link someone sent you. You will be prompted for your preferred nickname (and the password, if one was set).

## 💬 Mastery of Communication

Ephemeral Chat offers powerful tools built right into the message bar. Typing specific commands starting with `/` unlocks them instantly.

### ⌨️ Slash Commands Check-Sheet
Just type these directly into the chat box and hit Enter/Send:
*   `/camera` or `/photo` - Instantly take a photo or pick an image to share securely.
*   `/voice` or `/note` - Record an encrypted voice note.
*   `/poll` - Create an interactive, secure poll for everyone in the room to vote on.
*   `/pulse` - Send a physical device vibration to everyone in the room to get their attention!
*   `/ice` - Drops a random icebreaker question into the chat automatically.
*   `/media [youtube-url]` - Start a synchronized Watch Party. The video will play simultaneously for everyone in the room!

### 🎯 Targeted Messaging (Private Talk)
In a room with 10 people but only want to whisper to 2?
1.  Open the **User List** (tap the People icon in the top right).
2.  **Tap the Names** of the people you want to talk to. They will be highlighted.
3.  Go back to the chat. You'll see a blue bar saying **"Sending to X specific users"**.
4.  Send your message. **Only those selected people will receive the keys to decrypt it.** Everyone else gets nothing!
5.  *Tip: Tap "Clear selection" to go back to talking to everyone.*

### 📸 "View Once" Media
Photos can be toggled as **"View Once"**. Once the recipient views the photo and dismisses it, the decryption keys are shredded natively on their device. It's truly gone forever.

## 🛡️ Your Privacy Tools (Under the Hood)

We’ve built-in "magic" features to keep your chat truly private:

*   **🕵️ Privacy Blur (Instant Protection)**: The moment you switch apps or look away from your screen, the entire chat immediately obscures itself.
*   **👻 Ghost Watermarking**: You might notice faint, moving text hashed across the background of the chat. This acts to defeat artificial intelligence algorithms trying to perform character recognition from unauthorized snipers/cameras.
*   **🧨 Panic Burn**: If you are the Host and things go awry, triggering a Panic Burn sends a cryptographic kill switch to every connected device, instantly wiping the local chat state for all users.
*   **🔀 Traffic Padding**: Your app is continuously sending fake, randomized data to the server at erratic intervals. If your Internet provider tries to packet sniff your data, they will only see "noise"—they cannot even tell *when* you are actually sending a message, let alone what it contains.

## 📥 Installation: An App-Like Experience

Ephemeral Chat runs directly from your browser, but you can "install" it for a native, full-screen experience that works completely offline.

*   **iPhone/iPad (iOS)**: Tap the "Share" icon (the square with an arrow pointing up) in Safari and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
*   **Android**: Look for a prompt that says "Install App" or tap the browser menu (⋮) and hit "Add to Home Screen".
*   **Desktop**: Click the "Install" or "App" icon usually located on the far right of the address bar in Chrome/Edge/Brave.

## ❓ FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q: Why can't I see my own password when I type it to create/join?**  
**A:** This is "Stealth Entry." It prevents anyone physically nearby or a screen recorder from deducing your password string length. Just type confidently!

**Q: Do my messages stay on your server?**  
**A:** Never. Our server operates purely in active RAM. When the last person leaves a room, the RAM drops the session entirely. Furthermore, everything is end-to-end encrypted; the server only ever receives scrambled bytes it has no key to decode.

**Q: I sent a message to someone offline, but they didn't get it when they came back?**  
**A:** Ephemeral Chat is hyper-synchronous. If a device is totally disconnected from the session without establishing an active ratcheted keyshare, it cannot decrypt past messages when re-joining. This is entirely by design to safeguard your history from unauthorized joining.
